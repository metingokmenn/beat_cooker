Private by design

Your songs stay yours.

Beat Cooker is built by an independent developer who believes creative tools shouldn’t cost you your privacy.

  • No account, no email. The app signs you in anonymously — no name, email or phone number required.
  • No ads, no tracking. There are no advertising or tracking SDKs in the app, and it never asks for tracking permission.
  • Not used for training. Your prompts and lyrics are used only to create the songs you ask for.
  • Secure payments. Purchases are handled by Apple — we never see your card details.
  • Delete any time. Delete your account in the app and your songs and data are erased right away.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to create an account?

No. Beat Cooker creates an anonymous account for you automatically the first time you open the app. Your coins, purchases and songs are linked to it. Because it isn’t tied to an email address, deleting the app or changing devices may cause it to be lost.

How do coins work?

Creating a song uses coins. You can buy coin packs in the app, and a Beat Cooker PRO subscription includes coins with every billing period. If a song fails to generate, its coins are returned automatically. Coins have no cash value and can’t be transferred.

Are my lyrics used to train AI?

No. Your prompts, lyrics and songs are used only to create the content you request. We don’t use them to train our own AI models. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I ask for a song in the style of a specific artist?

Describe the sound instead — the mood, the tempo, the instruments. Beat Cooker doesn’t imitate specific artists: if a prompt names a real artist or song, the name is replaced with a description of that style before the song is made.

Is there anything I can’t make?

Yes. Prompts, lyrics and cover art are checked automatically, and requests for sexual, hateful, violent or otherwise harmful content are refused without using your coins. If a song you made still turns out objectionable, you can report it from its screen. The full rules are in our Terms of Use.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed in your App Store account settings (Settings › your name › Subscriptions). Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings › Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it: your coins, your songs and their cover art. On an older version of the app, or no longer have it? Email venusapp.tech@gmail.com and we’ll delete your data within 30 days.
